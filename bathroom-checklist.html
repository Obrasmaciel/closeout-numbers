Bathroom Remodel Estimate Checklist

Every line a full bathroom gut usually needs — and the ones that get left out.

Free. No email required. Built for the estimator pricing a 5x8 to 10x12 residential bathroom down to the studs.

How to use this

Price your bathroom the way you normally do. Then read this against it. You are not adding all of it — you are looking for the four or five lines that make you stop.

The items marked often missed are the ones that don't appear in a fixture-count estimate and show up as your problem in week three.

The number worth more than this list

Price your next bathroom by phase. At closeout, write down what each phase actually cost. Do it for five bathrooms.

You will find you are not randomly wrong — you are consistently light in the same one or two phases, by roughly the same percentage. That figure is specific to your crew, your subs and your market, and no checklist on the internet can hand it to you.
