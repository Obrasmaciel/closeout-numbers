Kitchen Remodel Estimate Checklist

Every line a full kitchen usually needs — and the ones that get left out.

Free. No email required. Built for the estimator pricing a residential kitchen from a light pull-and-replace to a full gut with a layout change.

How to use this

Price the kitchen the way you normally do, then read this against it. You are not adding all of it. You are looking for the four or five lines that make you stop — those are the ones that were going to come out of your margin.

Items marked often missed don't appear in a cabinet-and-countertop estimate and become your problem in week three.

The number worth more than this list

Price your next kitchen by phase. At closeout, write down what each phase actually cost. Do it for five kitchens.

You will find you are not randomly wrong — you are consistently light in the same one or two phases, by roughly the same percentage. That figure belongs to your crew, your subs and your market, and no checklist can hand it to you.
